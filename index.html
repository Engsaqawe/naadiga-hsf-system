Annual Programs

We are scholarship students giving back

The club brings together students who are currently pursuing their education through the Hormuud Salaam Foundation scholarship program, as well as graduates who have previously benefited from the scholarship. It serves as a platform for members to connect, share experiences, support one another academically and professionally, and contribute to the growth of a strong and collaborative community. Through networking, mentorship, and various educational activities, the club aims to strengthen the bond among scholarship beneficiaries while promoting learning, leadership, and personal development.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To strengthen the connection between students, graduates, and the institution while supporting academic and professional growth. The club promotes collaboration, community service, and the preservation of religious values, culture, and social harmony for the benefit of the country and its people.

Our Vision

To develop knowledgeable, creative, and responsible Somali students who contribute to the progress of their communities and the nation. The club seeks to foster education, innovation, and leadership to help build a sustainably developed and prosperous Somalia.

Cleaning Our Beaches, Protecting Our Environment

Every month, our volunteers restore Liido Beach removing waste and raising awareness for a cleaner Somalia.

Sharing Joy on Eid

We provide new clothing for underprivileged children, ensuring every child celebrates Eid with dignity and happiness. Over 400 children received gifts last year.

Feeding Hearts in Ramadan

During the holy month, we organize community iftars and distribute meals to hundreds of families. A moment of unity and compassion.

Monthly Meetings

We empower young minds through leadership workshops and mentorship programs. Knowledge shared is knowledge multiplied.

Summer Courses

We get free summer courses organized by hormuud salaam foundation for students, helping them prepare for a brighter future , these programs offer students essential skills and professional mentorship at no cost.
